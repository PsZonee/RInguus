HOW TO USE
1. Open index.html in any browser (double-click). Works on phones too.
2. Edit js/config.js: her name, your name, your date, letters, reasons, the big question.
3. Sorry page: js/sorry.js holds 1000 messages. Edit freely. "Email this" builds an email from the page you are on.
4. To put it online for free, drag this folder onto netlify.com/drop.
